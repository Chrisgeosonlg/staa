Common questions

Before you get in touch

Can STAA represent us before the Tanzania Revenue Authority?

Yes. We provide tax audit support and representation before TRA, alongside preparation and filing of corporate, individual, VAT and PAYE returns.

Do you work with businesses outside Tanzania?

Yes. We advise on doing business within the East African Community, including cross-border setup, tax considerations and regulatory compliance across regional jurisdictions.

Do you only work with large companies?

No. We serve SMEs, startups and entrepreneurs, corporates, NGOs and development partners, government and public-sector entities, and individual professionals.

Will our financial information be kept confidential?

Yes. Client confidentiality is one of the four principles behind every STAA engagement, together with accuracy, timely delivery and tailored solutions.

Can you prepare IFRS financial statements?

Yes. We prepare financial statements in line with IFRS standards, as well as bookkeeping, payroll, reporting and budgeting support.
