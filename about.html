About STAA

Financial clarity for a fast-moving region.

A professional financial services firm based in Dar es Salaam, helping organisations across Tanzania and East Africa achieve compliance, clarity and sustainable growth.

Who we are

Local expertise with a regional perspective.

Smile Tax & Accounting Advisory (STAA) provides comprehensive tax, accounting and business advisory services to individuals, SMEs, corporates, NGOs and government-related entities.

Operating within the dynamic and evolving East African business environment, we are committed to supporting organisations in achieving financial clarity, regulatory compliance and sustainable growth. We understand the regulatory landscape, tax systems and business challenges unique to the region, which lets us deliver solutions that are practical, compliant and growth-driven.
